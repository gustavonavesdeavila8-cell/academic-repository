# Academic Repository & Knowledge Base

A curated public collection of academic literature, research files, and structured study notes grounded in verified sources from top-tier Brazilian and international institutions.

---

**Directory Directory**
* 📄 **[Docs](./Docs)** — Theoretical reference documents, economic files, and literature.
* 🛠️ **[Projects](./Projects)** — Applied academic initiatives, research projects, and practical repositories.
* 📚 **[Study](./Study)** — Specialized notes and deep dives across disciplines (e.g., Biology, Immunology).

---

**Repository Architecture**
* **`Docs/`**: Core reference materials, theoretical frameworks, and archived academic papers.
* **`Projects/`**: Practical applications, research builds, and structured project files.
* **`Study/`**: Subject-by-subject study guides, lecture summaries, and analytical breakdowns.

---

**Curation Standards (Tier 1)**
1. **Academic Validation**: Content drawn exclusively from peer-reviewed publications, university syllabi, and established academic authorities.
2. **Conceptual Precision**: High-fidelity summaries with transparent citations and zero fluff.
3. **Structured Taxonomy**: Uniform directory layout for quick cross-referencing and study efficiency.
