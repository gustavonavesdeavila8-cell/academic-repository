* **Scientific Methodology**: Writing guides, analytical reading frameworks, and research techniques.

---

**Curation Standards (Tier 1)**
To maintain maximum academic rigor, all included materials adhere to three core pillars:
1. **Academic Validation**: Sourced directly from peer-reviewed publications or established university syllabi.
2. **Precision & Integrity**: Strict fidelity to original concepts with explicit citations.
3. **Standardized Formatting**: Clean, accessible documentation organized in structured Markdown and PDF formats.
